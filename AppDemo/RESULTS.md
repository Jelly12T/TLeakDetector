### What worked across languages

- Objective-C screens are marked and reported like Swift ones: block property (L01), strong delegate (L02, cycle
  only), NSTimer target (L04, `held by Timer` + `timerTarget`), block observer (L06, certain cycle through
  `__NSObserver.block` + `observerBlock`), suppression of an ObjC type (L13).
- Cross-language cycles L15, L16, L17 are certain cycles at T1; L16 also gets its warning.
- Budgets work for ObjC: `track` called from ObjC through the Swift shim (L38) and an ObjC child VC counted at
  `viewDidLoad` (L40). `allowedTypes` with an ObjC class (L55) and `LeakOptOut` declared on an ObjC class in a
  Swift extension (L56) are honoured.
- Root replacements: window `rootViewController` (L68, `replaceRoot`) and navigation `setViewControllers` (L69).
- A Swift Task started for an ObjC screen is reported as an outliving `Task` (L72).
- All weak/unowned controls (L03, L05, L07, L12, L18, L19, L27, L36, L46, L53, L54, L62, L64, L67), KVO not
  removed (L09) and the 4 s `dispatch_after` (L10) are silent.
